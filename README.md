
# HDRezka Client

A client for conveniently watching movies and TV series from the popular HDRezka website.

## About the Project

HDRezka Client provides a simple and functional way to access a vast library of movies and TV series without using a browser. Designed for comfortable viewing on a variety of devices.

## Key Features

* **Extensive Catalog:** Always an up-to-date catalog of movies, TV series, cartoons, and anime.
* **Flexible Quality Selection:** Choose your video quality, including automatic selection of the highest available quality.
* **Download Function:** Download content for offline viewing.
* **Favorites and History:** Add your favorite content to your favorites and save your viewing history with import/export functionality.
* **Dark Theme:** Comfortable viewing in the dark.
* **Optimized for Large Screens:** Supports tall displays and Android TV.

## Download and Installation

The latest version of the app can always be found on the [releases page](https://github.com/Niharoshka/hdrezka_client_release/releases):

1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com/Niharoshka/hdrezka_client_release/releases)** tab.
2. Download the latest available **.apk** file or other installation file.
3. Install the app on your device.
